Privacy Policy

PAPO'City: Zombie Car Survival · Effective date: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Robosoft ("we", "us") handles information when you play the mobile game PAPO'City: Zombie Car Survival (the "Game") on Android. We have built the Game so that we collect as little information as possible.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no user accounts, no login and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, gems, fuel, upgrades and settings such as sound) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data deletes this information permanently.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows optional rewarded video ads ("WATCH VIDEO" buttons) through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (for example, which ads were shown and whether a video was watched to the end).

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads depending on your device).

4. Crash and performance data (Unity)

The Game is built with the Unity engine. When the Game crashes or stops responding, Unity Technologies may collect diagnostic information such as the crash log, device model, operating system version and app version, so that we can find and fix problems. This information does not identify you personally. See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

5. Local multiplayer

The Game's multiplayer mode connects two devices directly over a local network using an IP address that the players enter themselves. Gameplay information and the devices' IP addresses are exchanged only between the connected devices for the length of the session. We do not run a game server and do not receive or store this information.

6. How information is shared

We do not sell your information. The only parties that receive information from the Game are the service providers named above (Google AdMob and Unity Technologies), for the purposes described in this policy. Information sent by the Game is encrypted in transit.

7. Children

The Game contains cartoon violence and advertising and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.
